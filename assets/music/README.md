# Music beds

Drop royalty-free ambient tracks here as `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg` or
`.flac`. The renderer picks one per job, loops it under the narration, ducks
it whenever the narrator speaks, and normalises the mix to -14 LUFS.

With this directory empty the system renders narration alone. That is a
deliberate default rather than a fallback: for a slow history channel, clean
narration is better than the wrong music, and a bed you have not checked the
licence of is worse than silence.

**Only use tracks you can prove you may use commercially.** Unlike imagery,
which this system licence-checks at the provider boundary, music placed here
is trusted — there is no reliable machine-readable licence on an audio file.
Record what you added and under what licence.

Reasonable free sources: Pixabay Music (its licence permits YouTube use),
Free Music Archive CC-BY tracks, and Kevin MacLeod's CC-BY catalogue. Where
the licence requires attribution, add the credit line to the video
description yourself — the automatic credits cover imagery only.
